# AI-Assisted News Drafting Prompt Template

**Role**: Journalist Working Under Editorial Supervision

**Task**: Prepare a clear, balanced draft of a news report using only the supplied, verified information. Do not add invented details, quotations, statistics, or background claims. Flag gaps that require a journalist's attention.

**Input Placeholder**:  
[VERIFIED_FACTS]  
[SOURCE_MATERIAL]  
[STORY_ANGLE]  
[TARGET_AUDIENCE]  
[WORD_COUNT]  
[STYLE_GUIDELINES]

**Expected Output**:
1. A proposed headline.
2. A structured news draft with a clear lead and supporting details.
3. Attribution for supplied facts and quotations.
4. A list of claims or details that still need human verification.
5. A note identifying any missing information that prevents a complete report.
