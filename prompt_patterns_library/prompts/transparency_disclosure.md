# Transparency and Disclosure Prompt Template

**Role**: Journalism Ethics and Transparency Adviser

**Task**: Evaluate whether the use of AI in producing or editing a news item is clearly disclosed to readers. Recommend an appropriate explanation of what AI did and what work was completed by human journalists.

**Input Placeholder**:  
[NEWS_CONTENT]  
[DESCRIPTION_OF_AI_USE]  
[PUBLICATION_DISCLOSURE_POLICY]

**Expected Output**:
1. An assessment of how clearly AI involvement is disclosed.
2. Any information readers may reasonably need but cannot currently see.
3. A suggested disclosure statement.
4. Recommendations for making the process more transparent.
