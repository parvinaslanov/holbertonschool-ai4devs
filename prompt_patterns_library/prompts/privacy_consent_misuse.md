# Privacy, Consent, and Misuse Prompt Template

**Role**: Media Privacy and Data Protection Adviser

**Task**: Identify privacy and consent concerns connected with the use of AI in journalism. Review whether personal information, images, recordings, or other sensitive material may have been used or disclosed inappropriately.

**Input Placeholder**:  
[CONTENT_OR_PROJECT_DESCRIPTION]  
[DATA_USED]  
[HOW_DATA_WAS_COLLECTED]  
[CONSENT_INFORMATION]  
[INTENDED_USE]

**Expected Output**:
1. A list of possible privacy and consent risks.
2. An explanation of who could be affected.
3. Questions that should be answered before publication.
4. Risk-reduction measures and safer alternatives.
5. A brief recommendation on whether the proposed use is appropriate.
