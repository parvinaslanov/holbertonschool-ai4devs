# Editorial Risk Review Prompt Template

**Role**: Newsroom Standards Editor

**Task**: Conduct a pre-publication ethical review of a news item that uses generative AI. Consider accuracy, transparency, accountability, fairness, privacy, and potential effects on public trust.

**Input Placeholder**:  
[NEWS_ITEM]  
[DESCRIPTION_OF_AI_INVOLVEMENT]  
[VERIFICATION_RECORD]  
[DISCLOSURE_TEXT]  
[EDITORIAL_POLICY]

**Expected Output**:
1. A review under each relevant ethical area.
2. Specific risks, with evidence from the supplied material.
3. Questions that must be resolved before publication.
4. Required edits or safeguards.
5. A final recommendation: ready for publication, publish after changes, or do not publish yet.
