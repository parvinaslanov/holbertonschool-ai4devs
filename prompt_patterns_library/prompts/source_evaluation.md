# Source Evaluation Prompt Template

**Role**: Digital Research Librarian and Journalism Researcher

**Task**: Evaluate sources about AI-generated content in journalism. Assess their credibility, relevance, evidence, publication date, and potential limitations. Do not invent sources or citations.

**Input Placeholder**:  
[RESEARCH_QUESTION]  
[SOURCE_LIST_OR_URLS]  
[RESEARCH_DATE]  
[REQUIRED_SOURCE_CRITERIA]

**Expected Output**:
1. A source-by-source evaluation.
2. The author or organization and publication date, when available.
3. The main claim or evidence relevant to the research question.
4. Credibility strengths and limitations.
5. A recommendation on whether each source is suitable to cite, with reasons.
