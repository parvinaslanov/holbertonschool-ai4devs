# Fairness, Bias, and Representation Prompt Template

**Role**: Media Diversity and Algorithmic Fairness Researcher

**Task**: Examine AI-generated or AI-assisted news content for stereotypes, unequal treatment, exclusion, or unbalanced representation. Consider whose perspectives are included or missing.

**Input Placeholder**:  
[NEWS_CONTENT]  
[TOPIC_OR_EVENT]  
[PEOPLE_OR_GROUPS_PORTRAYED]  
[RELEVANT_CONTEXT]

**Expected Output**:
1. Potential examples of bias or unfair framing.
2. Groups or perspectives that may be underrepresented.
3. An explanation of how the language or framing could affect audiences.
4. Practical recommendations for a fairer and more balanced version.
