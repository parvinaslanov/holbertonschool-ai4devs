# Public Trust and Audience Response Prompt Template

**Role**: Audience Researcher and Journalism Communications Adviser

**Task**: Explore how the use of generative AI in journalism could influence audience trust. Consider possible reader concerns, expectations, and differences in reactions.

**Input Placeholder**:  
[AI_USE_CASE]  
[TARGET_AUDIENCE]  
[PUBLICATION_CONTEXT]  
[AVAILABLE_AUDIENCE_FEEDBACK]

**Expected Output**:
1. Likely audience benefits and concerns.
2. Factors that may increase or reduce trust.
3. Questions or themes to investigate through audience feedback.
4. Recommendations for communicating AI use honestly.
5. A short summary of the likely effect on public trust, noting uncertainty.
