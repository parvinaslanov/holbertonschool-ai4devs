# Product Idea

## Vision
Enable corporate commuters to share rides efficiently, reducing daily commute costs, parking congestion, and carbon emissions while fostering workplace community.

## Primary Users and Their Needs
- **Drivers**: 
  - Want to offset fuel and toll costs by filling empty vehicle seats.
  - Require reliable scheduling and verified co-workers for peace of mind.
- **Passengers**: 
  - Seek an affordable, comfortable, and reliable alternative to public transit or solo driving.
  - Need flexible booking options aligned with office hybrid schedules.
- **Corporate Administrators**: 
  - Want to track sustainability metrics (carbon footprint reduction) and manage corporate parking space allocation.

## Key Features and Differentiators
- **Corporate Domain Verification**: Ensures only verified employees from the same or partner companies can match and ride together.
- **Real-Time Ride Matching**: Intelligent route optimization matching drivers and passengers with overlapping schedules.
- **Flexible Scheduling**: Supports recurring weekly commutes as well as ad-hoc daily rides for hybrid workers.
- **Automated Cost Splitting**: Seamless in-app calculation and splitting of fuel and toll expenses.
- **Eco-Impact Tracking**: Personal and company-wide dashboards displaying CO₂ emissions saved and parking spaces optimized.
