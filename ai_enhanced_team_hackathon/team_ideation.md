## Problem Statement
Non-technical users often struggle to understand, diagnose, and resolve everyday computer and IT issues—ranging from Windows and driver errors to BIOS/UEFI, hardware, and network failures—resulting in unnecessary frustration, downtime, and reliance on costly technical support.

## MVP Features
- **Plain-Language Problem Input:** Users describe their computer or IT issue in simple, everyday terms without needing technical jargon.
- **Root Cause Analysis:** AI analyzes symptoms across hardware, Windows, BIOS/UEFI, drivers, and network layers to identify the most likely causes.
- **Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Guide:** Generates clear, sequential, beginner-friendly instructions to safely resolve the issue.
- **Interactive Diagnostic Flow:** Asks targeted follow-up questions to narrow down complex problems when initial symptoms are broad.
- *(Future Scope)* **Error Log & Screenshot Analysis:** Upload error messages or photos of BSOD/BIOS screens for instant visual diagnosis.
